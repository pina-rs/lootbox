# Scripts for your lootbox.so video

Three scripts for you to read on camera as the person who built lootbox.so. Each one lines up with the `CreatorCut` edit template in this app. The **[B-roll]** cues name the animated inserts that cut in over your voice, so you never need to demo the product while you talk.

Pick one to start. **Script B** is the main launch video. **Script A** is the fast hook for the timeline. **Script C** is the one most likely to get replies, because it's a real giveaway.

Read the lines in your own words. The rhythm matters more than the exact wording: short sentences, a beat of silence where there's a `/`, and energy on the **bold** words.

---

## Script A — "The hook" (15 seconds, vertical)

For: the first post, a pinned tweet, or a reply to anyone asking what lootbox is.

| Time    | You say                                                                                      | On screen                                       |
| ------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| 0–3 s   | "I built a way to put **real prizes** inside a box / that anyone can open."                  | You, close-up. Caption pops in word by word.    |
| 3–7 s   | "SOL, memecoins, stock tokens, NFTs. / They're locked **on chain** before anyone opens one." | **[B-roll: Lock]** prizes fly into the chest.   |
| 7–11 s  | "Send boxes to your friends, / or put them on a **curve** and let people trade them."        | **[B-roll: Hand out]** airdrop and curve split. |
| 11–15 s | "Hold the chest. / See what's inside. / **lootbox.so**."                                     | **[B-roll: Open]** chest pops, then end card.   |

## Script B — "Why I built lootbox" (45–60 seconds, horizontal)

For: the launch post. This is the one to pin.

**1. Hook (0–6 s).** "Everyone loves opening a lootbox. / Nobody trusts **what's inside** one." _On screen: you, medium shot. Caption "Nobody trusts what's inside."_

**2. The idea (6–16 s).** "So I built **lootbox.so**. / You fill a box with real prizes: / SOL, memecoins, even tokenized stocks. / And every single prize is locked on chain / **before** anyone opens a box." _[B-roll: Fill] bundles pop into the builder; the odds strip reflows as copies change._

**3. Fair by construction (16–26 s).** "One box is one ticket. / The odds are just the prizes you put in, / there for everyone to see. / And the draw uses verifiable randomness, / so not even I can pick the winner." _[B-roll: Odds] the odds strip, "1 in 20" highlighted. Caption: "Not even I can pick the winner."_

**4. Hand them out (26–40 s).** "Then you choose how they go out. / Paste a list of wallets and **airdrop** them. / You cover the fees, so it's free to receive. / Or put them on a **curve**: / every box costs a little more than the last, / and people can sell them back until it sells out." _[B-roll: Hand out] the airdrop list fires boxes at wallets, then the curve staircase fills up and sells out._

**5. Nobody leaves empty-handed (40–48 s).** "And if your box isn't the big one, / you still get an **Exclusive Lootbox NFT**, / with its own rarity." _[B-roll: Exclusive] a fan of chests in different finishes; a rare one sparkles._

**6. Call to action (48–56 s).** "It's live at **lootbox.so**. / Make one for your community, / and tell me what you put inside." _End card: the logo. The chest looks at the word, and the word looks back._

For: the post that gets people to open a box and tell their friends. It's a free giveaway: you airdrop the boxes, so nobody pays to play.

| Time    | You say                                                                                 | On screen                                                    |
| ------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 0–3 s   | "I just put **1 SOL** in a lootbox."                                                    | You holding your phone to camera. Caption: "1 SOL. One box." |
| 3–8 s   | "There are 20 boxes. / One has the SOL. / The rest are memecoins and collectible NFTs." | **[B-roll: Fill]** the three bundles and the odds strip.     |

| 14–20 s | "Reply with your wallet / and I'll **airdrop** you a box. / Free. I pay the fees."      | **[B-roll: Airdrop]** list, boxes flying out.                |

Before you post it: fund the box, set the reveal date, and make sure the reply-to-claim rules are clear and lawful where you are. A free draw with no purchase is the safe shape.

## Recording at home

**Framing.** Record horizontal (16:9), 4K if your camera or phone can, with your head and shoulders centred. The edit crops the same take to vertical 9:16, so keep your hands and face inside the middle third of the frame. Leave a little headroom.

**Look.** Face a window, or put a lamp behind the camera and slightly to one side. Plain or tidy background, at least a metre behind you, so the edit can blur it.

**Sound matters most.** A phone on a stand one arm's length away is fine; a clip-on mic is better. Turn off fans and the fridge if you can. Record **10 seconds of silence** at the start of the session (room tone), then **clap once** on camera before the first take so audio and video line up.

**Takes.** Do each section of the script two or three times in a row without stopping the recording. Pause for a breath between takes. The edit picks the best one of each. Mistakes are fine; just pause and repeat the line.

**Energy.** Talk to one friend through the lens, a little faster and brighter than feels natural. Smile on the first line and on the call to action.

**When you're done,** drop the files (and which script you read) into `apps/promo/public/footage/` or send them over. The edit adds captions, the animated inserts, music, sound effects, and the end card.
